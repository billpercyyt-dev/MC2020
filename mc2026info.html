Info/Tips

The aim of the game is to climb to the top of the Mount by jumping on the coloured blocks.

The Red blocks have a strange anti-gravitational force which propels you to jump two blocks at a time.

Try and avoid anything that moves around.

Use the arrow keys on your laptop to move left, right, up or down.

The Story

Many years ago, in the last century, ... well, 1985, to be precise, a strange object emerged from the depths of the South Pacific Ocean. No one knew what to make of it, in fact, even the scientific community were extremely wary of it. It was covered in green and red blocks which radiated strange pulses. There were also wild, alien, animals and plants to be found all over the granite-type surface. Not until 1987 did anyone dare make an attempt to explore, what was now named, 'Mount Challenge'. An eccentric professor conquered the Mount but, after he returned, the tales he recounted seemed so far fetched that the media surmised it was all a hoax and consequently Mount Challenge gradually faded in the public's memory.

So this is where you come in. During covid, word spread that major changes had been observed on the Mount. Although they won't set foot on it, the inhabitants of the islands, which are the closest to Mount Challenge, said the wild life has been decimated by some sort of virus. The fishermen who fish in the waters nearby even changed its name to Mount Corona*. Your job is to conquer Mount Challenge and report back your findings.

[*Mount Challenge 2020 was coded in the Java programming language during the covid lockdown. In 2026, it has been re-designed and re-written (in Javascript) and hosted on the 'Billy's Doodles' website.]
